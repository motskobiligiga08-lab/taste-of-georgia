Georgia by region

Each part of the country has its own landscape, climate, and kitchen.

Kakheti

The heart of Georgian wine. Endless vineyards, ancient qvevri cellars, and long supra tables filled with generosity.

Svaneti

High Caucasus towers and hearty mountain food. Kubdari, local cheeses, and wild herbs grown in the thin air.

Adjara

Black Sea coast and the famous boat-shaped Adjarian khachapuri finished with a golden egg yolk.
