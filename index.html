Territories

Georgia by region – Travel & Wine Guide

Each part of the country has its own landscape, climate, and kitchen. Discover the best places to visit in Georgia.

🍷

Kakheti – Georgian Wine Region

The heart of Georgian wine. Endless vineyards, ancient qvevri cellars, and long supra tables filled with generosity. Best place for wine tasting in Georgia.

Saperavi Rkatsiteli Churchkhela
🏔️

Svaneti – Mountain Georgia

High Caucasus towers and hearty mountain food. Kubdari, local cheeses, and wild herbs grown in the thin air. Perfect for hiking and culture.

Kubdari Svan salt Tashmijabi
🌊

Adjara – Black Sea Coast

Black Sea coast and the famous boat-shaped Adjarian khachapuri finished with a golden egg yolk. Great for summer travel in Georgia.

Adjarian Khachapuri Sinori Citrus
🌿

Racha-Lechkhumi

Forest-covered hills, smoked meats, and the rare semi-sweet Khvanchkara wine loved across the country. Hidden gem for Georgian food lovers.

Khvanchkara Lori Shkmeruli
Essentials

Georgian Food & Wine

A few dishes and wines that define Georgian hospitality and culture.

Must-try Georgian dishes

  • Khachapuri Cheese bread
  • Khinkali Soup dumplings
  • Mtsvadi Grilled meat
  • Pkhali Vegetable pâtés
  • Churchkhela Walnut candy

Essential Georgian wines

  • Saperavi Deep red
  • Rkatsiteli Amber / white
  • Khvanchkara Semi-sweet
  • Kindzmarauli Semi-sweet red
  • Tsinandali Classic white
